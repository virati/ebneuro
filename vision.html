Our Vision

Bridging Minds and Machines

We believe neurotechnology should serve people first. Our work centers on building open, accessible tools that give communities direct agency over how brain-computer interfaces are developed and deployed.

The East Bay has always been a place where grassroots innovation thrives. We draw from that tradition — merging rigorous neuroscience with community-driven engineering to create technologies that are transparent, equitable, and designed for the public good.

Our long-term goal is a future where neural interfaces are not controlled by a handful of corporations, but shaped by the people who use them. Open protocols. Shared data. Collective governance. Technology that understands the brain because it listens to the people behind it.
