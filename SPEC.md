# EastBay NeuroTech

Slogan: People, with Brains.
Philosophy: People-centric development of NeuroTech, with an emphasis on opensource and community-driven technologies.

## Design
The website should be clean, crisp, and follow modern design practices.

The center should be a network of nodes that drift slightly - look at labs.nform.ai for the general vibe and code.
In this website, the center figure should make the shape of a brain on the left, and of the Easy Bay silhouette on the right.
There should be connections between the two that mimic the corpus collosum.
